For the paper (Dataset section):
  "We use the arXiv metadata snapshot from Kaggle (Cornell University; latest record dated 2026-10-30;
   3,233,620 papers). We sample 125000 papers per domain (D1=cs, D2=math, D3=astro-hep, D4=condmat-quant) with seed 42 and extract
   at most 20 keywords per paper with RAKE (AMTP Phase 1)."
